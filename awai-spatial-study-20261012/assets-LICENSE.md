This is a Blender-rendered camera and spatial composition study created for AWAI. All geometry, lighting and footage were authored locally for this study. No customer website, prior project, stock texture or HDR image is used.

The rendered glyphs use Windows Arial. The font is not distributed. The review interface loads Archivo and IBM Plex Sans JP via Google Fonts under their SIL Open Font Licenses.

This film does not establish the rendering performance of a realtime 3D website.
